Saarek Lesse is a [[Human]] From long ago born in the New Wilds

# Personality

Saarek acts as a tough cookie, but he's actually a big softie who just wants to be shown compassion. 

His main ambition is to atone for the sins he's committed against the world

He fights for Rievyss as that's the only way he sees to atone.

## Traits


### Ideals
Compassion is just a manipulation tactic to trick you into being usable by others.
### Bonds
I wish to save my brother, even if it means my life becomes empty.
### Flaws 
My own life means nothing. If I could end it to save everyone else I would.

# History

## Birth and Early Life
Saarek Lesse was born over 250 Years ago to the noble Lesse family in the New Wilds. While his mom, Sandra Lesse was pregnant with him she was unable to use magic or even retain mana. they tried to abort him but it failed due to his mutation.

After Saarek's birth his family ignored him and just let their servants care for the baby. Saarek, having been raised by the servants saw how rude his family was to them which always upset him

At age 6, Saarek's parents realized he would never be able to use magic. At which point they started beating him, to try and "fix his defective body" eventually the beatings caught up and he died. But he then respawned in his bed. after realizing that wouldn't work. They just used the boy as target practice for their spells. His elder brother Michael Lesse Jr. Also abused him and made a mockery of him. It wasn't until at age 13, when his brother Dracon Lesse was born that he felt any sense of purpose.

## Teen Years

In his Teens, Saarek enlisted into the Military of Hume. He also protected his younger brother from harassment by Michael Jr. After an encounter with a military mage who specialized in magical barriers, Saarek realized he could absorb magic from spells as well as the natural world. Which then enhances his physical power. When his brother Dracon went off to college, Saarek helped see him off from the shadows

## the Dragon's Curse
In his 30's Saarek was given a mission to steal an ancient relic from a dragon's hoard. The dragon caught him, and after failing to Curse Saarek due to his mutation, He ended up cursing His closest Relative, Dracon Lesse.

## Saarek's Greatest Failure
After his brother was cursed. Saarek tried to help him. But the curse proved to be too tightly bound to Dracon's soul. Even with Saarek's ability to see and interact with magic he couldn't rid his brother of the curse. Nore did his brother trust him anymore. After they fell out, Saarek left the Hume army and became a nomad, travelling the dimensions to find help for his brother

## The Dracon Incident
Saarek feels great regret for the part he played in causing the Dracon Incident, and Hates himself every day for what he's done.

## The Price to Pay
Saarek makes a deal with a spirit and loses 3 of his sense to make the blades Hate and Regret. And to ease his brother's suffering

## Modern Day
Saarek Works for Rievyss, along side Amanita Dusk, Fedir Nikolaev, And the Nuclear Automaton
