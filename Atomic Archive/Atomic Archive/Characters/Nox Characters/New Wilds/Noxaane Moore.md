# Personality
Noxaane is a kind hearted girl who's afraid to hurt people.

She wants to save cyberron without violence, because she hates to see people in pain. 

Noxaane is emotionally volatile but tries her best to keep them in check, often failing and causing problems.

# History
## birth
Noxaane is the daughter of Dracon & Stringen Moore. She was not born through biological means but through magical means. The method of her birth gives her a unique sensitivity to magic and spirits. Especially chaos magic. She's been an aetherborne since her birth which caused problems for her parents

## early life
As a kid, Noxaane was always fascinated by the moons and it's phases. This is due to her body and soul's unique attunement to the moon. As a child she had access to 2 phases of the moon to align with. The New moon and the Full Moon

The New moon allowed her to turn invisible and become intangible, making her unable to be touched or to touch others. 

The full moon causes her to glow with a pale light. Shedding dim light in 5 feet. And causes her to take and deal half damage from/to all sources.

## the horrors begin (teen years)

As she grew up she gained more phases of the moon. As well as learning about what her fathers do for work. She was never allowed in Dracon's workshop but one day, when she was 13, dracon forgot to lock up his workshop due to how tired he was. And noxaane, being the curious daughter of an inventor went in there and found thrikull, the bloodshed scythe. A Weapon whispering about slaughter and chaos. She was looking at the scythe in wonder and before she coulr even realize what she was doing, her hand had already touched the scythe. Visions of a field of slaughter, blood splashing onto the grass. A howl that deafened the ears. 

She was found sitting on the floor in Dracon's workshop crying the next morning by Stringen. When he found her Stringen learned what she saw and hugged her until she calmed down.

## After meeting Thrikull
After she calmed down Stringen asked her where the scythe was but she didn't know, when dracon entered the lab he freaked out and inspected her arm for where the smart metal scythe went. When they found it attached to her arm. Dracon realized his mistake and started training Noxaane with the help of Stringen in magic and martial combat.

## leaving the nest

When noxaane became 22 she left home to do some reconnaissance and research on what's become of cyberron. Being told to live there for 9 years and then report back her findings.

However when she got to cyberron and saw the injustice, she couldnt help but join a group of vigilantes as the Lunar Renegade.
