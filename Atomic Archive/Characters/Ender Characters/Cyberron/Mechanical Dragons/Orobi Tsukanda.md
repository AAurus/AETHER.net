## Orobi doesn't know how to read people

Orobi’s the kind of guy who likes parties and just. Interacting with people. He ends up making some friends at these  

[[Orobi's Ex-Friend|One friend]] is a guy who doesn’t exactly have orobi’s best interests in mind- only reason he even befriended Orobi was because of the tsukanda last name. He had some kind of thing against Nishi but I’m not sure what?  

Orobi at this point is kind of a naive kid. Nishi has told him that the world is dangerous and has insisted on the whole “you have to be strong to survive” thing but Orobi is overconfident and thinks that ‘since I’m a dragon I’m going to win the fight against some random guy if I try”  

To Orobi nothing is weird with this guy. To him if someone’s going to cause him harm, they’d just go straight out swinging. Plus this guy is a friend now! He’s not so bad! (Orobi has probably only known him for a few months at this point)   

When the guy ends up attacking Orobi, he ends up basically freezing in shock initially because he has no clue whats going on or why he’s doing this. During the attack Orobi sustains what would be fatal injuries to basically anyone that isn’t a robot- the main injuring being that his left eye got stomped in. Orobi’s power crystal can’t properly sustain him so he shuts down, and the guy assumes he’s dead and leaves  

CURRENT CONCEPT: Keina finds Orobi  I’m not sure where the whole thing happened, but maybe it’s somewhere near yamanaka metalworks?? Or maybe it’s near some tsukanda related building. I think that would make sense, since that could have been with the intent that Nishi might find him Keina finds an unconscious body in an alley somewhere and realizes it’s Orobi next to dead. But Orobi seems to stir slightly so Keina does his best to see if he’s okay- to which Orobi can barely reply on the account of being that badly beat up   

Keina helps Orobi back to the tsukanda apartment. He goes to contact Nishi and Orobi freaks out at the idea of him finding out because in the moment his mind jumps to what his father only told him and is scared that Nishi is going to be disappointed in him and thing he’s better off dead if this happened to him. Keina offers to stay until Nishi gets there, so if Nishi seems like he’s reacting badly Keina can try to step in, to which Orobi agrees as he at least has a bit of backup   

Nishi gets the call from Keina and immediately freaks out and returns home as fast as possible, terrified he’s going to lose someone else he cares about. Nishi goes full mechanic mode on Orobi trying to help him and Orobi basically breaks down in front of him because he was so sure that Nishi was going to be upset and Nishi just. Gets smacked with guilt because he realizes he’s been awful at being a father because he hasn’t been helping Orobi survive and is instead making Orobi afraid of him  

Orobi’s recovery is sped up by Nishi’s work but he’s lost an eye and basically sustained neurological damage- his balance is all fucked up and there’s at least one occasion where he falls over because he doesn’t realize his balance is like that. He ends up with basically an eyepatch temporarily as Nishi tries to figure out an eye replacement, because the mechanical dragon’s self-repair doesn’t exactly regrow an eye. While Orobi’s balance recovers he continues to experience what is essentially migraines- he gets awful pain behind his left eye occasionally.  

Honestly I think him surviving the whole thing is his divine feat that made him aetherborne  

Orobi eventually recovers enough and realizes he wants to actually learn how to fight, so that if anything like this happens again he doesn’t just freeze up and basically take it until he literally cannot fight back.  

At this point I’m not sure if he gets professional training or what? But eventually he realizes that he is actually really interested in fighting, and it ends up leading to Nishi having the whole idea of the death ring- give his kid a chance to fight as a job if that’s what he likes.
