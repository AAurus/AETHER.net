## Desc
Survival is based on luck, not just skill. This is what Stígandr believes. But it wasn’t luck that put them in the uphill battle they fight. Created to be a servant to a master that abandoned them while fleeing persecution, they were found by a wizard whose research involved dabbling in human experimentation- and what better test subject than one created to mimic humans, without many of their flaws? 

Stígandr is now one of the few survivors from the previous generation of Aetherbornes, but their state as an Aetherborne is… dubious. They aren’t sure if they actually have a soul, and don’t consider themself a person- but they were recognized by the gods of the land they ended up in, and are therefore an Aetherborne. They know that in the event of their death, they’ll find their answer… but for now, they don’t want to know.

At the same time, the strain the modifications have created is causing their body to slowly erode. Their only hope to stop this is the promise Upsilon made to fix them. But they don’t know if he can make good on that promise.

## Backstory

Long ago, Herleif and Eyvindr were among Ysgard's brightest minds: alchemists whose skills reached that of being able to create life. Despite being acquainted, the two rarely worked together. If asked, either of them would blame a difference in ideals. Despite this, Herleif was willing to give Eyvindr the first look at her finished Homunculus.

Herleif's creation was a creature created to be her servant. One that followed her orders, and fulfilled the commands given.

Eyvindr was impressed, but certain factors about the Homunculus concerned him. For a being created as an obedient servant, he found it odd how person-like they were in appearance and movement. They didn't act as a person would- they only followed commands. The servant was also given a name. Stígandr. He found it odd for a being so person-like to not have personhood. But beyond mentioning it to Herleif, he did not push it further. It was clear they were not fully conscious, and while it felt odd to him, he saw no reason to step in.

But Eyvindr was not aware of how easily insulted Herleif was.

While Stígandr was aware of what they had to do when given a command, they were not consciously aware of what they were doing. So when Herleif gave them a command, Herleif intended to be the last she gave- they did not know. The command was simple: kill Eyvindr.

And it was an easy task.
