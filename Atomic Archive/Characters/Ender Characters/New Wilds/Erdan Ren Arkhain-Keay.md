#Endercoil 
**Erdan Arkhain-Keay** is an [[Elf]] from the city of [[Mitsilveln]].


# History

Born to parents [[Achros Arkhain]] and [[Erdan Keay]], under the name Ren, Ren grew up in the city with both of their parents until [[Erdan Keay]]'s 'disappearance' during [[the dracon incident]]. Like many elves alive at the time, Ren's view was strongly shaped by the event and [[Aether Council|the Aether]]'s treatment of it, leading to them to inherently distrust the council.

Despite their passion for magic and interest in following in their parents footsteps, [[Erdan Keay|Erdan]]'s disappearance and assumed death, alongside [[Aeberos Altha]] becoming an [[Aetherborne]] lead Ren to follow other interests, instead pursuing acting as a career.
