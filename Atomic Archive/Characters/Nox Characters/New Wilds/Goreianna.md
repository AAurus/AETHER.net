# life before her mission
Goreianna is the daughter of a minotaur and the spirit of flight. Her birth was seen as a bad omen by the tribe she was born into so they threw her off a cliff and into the ocean. However at the bottom of the cliff was the spirit of anchors. Who say Goreianna's Birth as a golden opportunity to get at her rival spirit who was Goreianna's father. But as Goreianna grew up with the spirit of Anchors. they formed a Parent daughter relationship and eventually performed a ritual to make Goreianna a spiritborne so she would be safe. This combination of 2 spirits got the recognition of the Aether system and caused her to become an Aetherborne. 

If Goreianna begins to doubt who she is or strays too far from her comfort zone her body starts to crack from the stress.
